The problem

Your Real Estate Marketing Shouldn't Start From Scratch Every Time.

Every listing, social post, email, and market update creates another marketing task. Without a repeatable process, your messaging becomes inconsistent, content creation takes longer, and keeping everything aligned with your brand becomes difficult.

Inconsistent Branding

Your marketing looks and sounds different from one piece of content to the next.

Too Much Repetitive Work

Writing property descriptions, captions, and emails from scratch takes time away from other business priorities.

Disconnected Marketing

Each piece of content is created independently instead of supporting a consistent marketing approach.

No Repeatable Process

When every new listing or campaign requires a new starting point, marketing becomes harder to organize and maintain.

The MABS solution

One Brand System. Endless Marketing Possibilities.

MABS brings AI-powered content creation and real estate marketing workflows into one focused workspace, helping you create content around your brand, your business, and your properties.

  1. 01

    Create With Purpose

    Generate marketing content for real estate activities using specialized tools designed for specific content types and objectives.

  2. 02

    Stay Brand-Aligned

    Your Brand Context (name, brokerage, voice, service areas and contact details) guides every generator, so your content keeps one consistent voice.

  3. 03

    Build a Repeatable Workflow

    Enter a property's facts once in a project, then create the listing, the posts and the emails from the same details.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MABS?

MABS, or Megmos AI Brand Studio, is an AI-powered marketing and growth toolkit designed for real estate professionals. It provides specialized generators for property descriptions, social media content, emails, market updates, video scripts, blog posts, website pages and more.

Who can use MABS?

Independent real estate agents, luxury agents, real estate teams, and brokers looking for a more structured approach to marketing content creation.

What can I create with MABS?

Property and luxury listing descriptions, listing campaigns, flyer copy, Instagram captions, social posts, video scripts, market updates, neighborhood guides, blog posts, marketing emails, client follow-ups, lead nurture sequences, listing presentations, an agent bio and your website pages. Some generators are part of paid plans.

How does MABS work?

Set up your Brand Context once. Choose a generator, provide the requested information, and generate content that you can review, edit and adapt to your marketing needs.

Do I need technical experience?

No. MABS is form-based: you fill in the fields a generator asks for and click Generate. There is nothing to install.

Does MABS automatically publish content to my social media accounts?

No. MABS creates the content; you review it and publish it wherever you choose. It does not connect to or post on your social accounts.

Does MABS invent property details?

No. Generators use only the facts you provide, and numbers are copied exactly as you enter them. If a detail is missing, it is left out rather than made up.

Can I use MABS for luxury real estate?

Yes. MABS includes a Luxury Listing Description generator for a more refined tone, still built only from your property facts.

Which languages does MABS support?

English and Arabic, for both the interface and the content you generate.

How can I get started?

Create a free account. The free plan includes 10 AI generations a month and the Starter generators, with no credit card required. Paid plans with more generators and higher limits are coming soon.
