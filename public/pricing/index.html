Questions about pricing

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free with no credit card: the Starter generators and 10 AI generations a month, in English and Arabic.

What counts as an AI generation?

Each time you create content with a generator, including Regenerate. Editing, copying and downloading don't count. The count starts again on the 1st of each month.

How do video credits work?

One credit pays for up to 15 seconds of video, so a 60-second video uses 4 credits. If a video fails to render, its credits come back. Professional includes 4 credits a month and Elite 40.

How much do I save with yearly billing?

20%. Professional is $468 a year instead of $588, and Elite is $948 a year instead of $1,188.

What is the Brokerage plan?

For teams and offices that need more volume: everything in Elite, unlimited AI generations and 150 video credits a month. The price is agreed with each office, from $249 a month.

Does every plan work in Arabic?

Yes. Every plan, including Starter, works in English and Arabic, for both the app and the content you create.
