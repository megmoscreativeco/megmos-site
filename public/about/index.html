About

Built by real estate marketers, for real estate professionals.

MABS (Megmos AI Brand Studio) is made by Megmos Creative Co., a branding and real estate marketing studio. It turns the way we approach real estate marketing into a tool every agent can use.

Why we built MABS

Real estate marketing asks for the same thing again and again: a listing description, then posts, then emails, then a flyer, each written from scratch and each slightly different from the last.

We built MABS so agents can enter their brand and their property facts once, and create every piece of marketing from that one source. The result is faster work and a brand that sounds the same everywhere.

What we stand for

Facts stay facts

MABS uses only the facts you provide. Numbers are copied exactly. Nothing is invented: no features, prices or market statistics.

Your brand, every time

Your Brand Context guides every generator, so your voice and details stay consistent across listings, posts, emails and your website.

Fair-housing aware

Copy describes properties and places, never who should live there. You stay in control and review everything before it is published.

English and Arabic

MABS works in both languages, with proper right-to-left layouts, for agents serving international markets.

What MABS does today

  • 23 AI generators for listings, social media, email, video scripts, blog posts, market updates, your agent bio and your website pages.
  • Projects that hold a property's facts once, and Repurpose to turn one piece of content into another.
  • A Website Kit with all your website pages in one place, ready to copy, with SEO titles and descriptions.
  • A free plan with 10 AI generations a month, no credit card required.
