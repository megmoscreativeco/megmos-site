The workflow

Four Steps From Your Details to Your Draft.

Every generator in MABS follows the same path, so once you've used one, you know how they all work.

  1. 01

    Step 1 · Bring your brand context

    Start with Your Business

    Save your business details once in Brand Context, inside Settings. MABS makes them available to its generators, so you don't retype your name, voice or service areas for every piece. Brand Context is optional: you can generate content without completing it, and add to it at any time.

    Give MABS the context it needs. The more relevant the information, the better the starting point for your marketing content.

    Brand Context can include

    • Your business or agency name and brokerage
    • Your services and a short company description
    • Your target audience
    • Your brand voice and style
    • Your service areas
    • Contact details and social profiles
  2. 02

    Step 2 · Choose your generator

    Choose What You Need to Create

    MABS organizes marketing tasks into specialized generators, so you never start from a blank prompt. Each generator is built for one job, and your choice decides which fields you see and what kind of content you get.

    Whether you're preparing a property listing, writing a social caption, planning an email, or developing website copy, choose the workflow that matches your goal.

    For example

    • MLS Description (Included in the free plan)
    • Luxury Listing Description (Included in the free plan)
    • Instagram Caption (Included in the free plan)
    • Email Marketing (Included in the free plan)
    • Website Page Copy (Paid plans, coming soon)
    Included in the free planPaid plans, coming soon
  3. 03

    Step 3 · Add the relevant details

    Tell Us About the Property or Campaign

    Complete a short, structured form. Required fields are marked, and the rest are optional. When you work inside a project, the property facts you saved there are ready to use.

    Provide the facts that matter. MABS uses the information you supply to prepare a relevant first draft, while leaving you in control of what gets included.

    Depending on the generator, fields can include

    • Property address and type
    • Bedrooms, bathrooms and size
    • Key or standout features
    • Listing price, saved in your project
    • Post type, key message and call to action
    • Tone, and a focus keyword for website pages

    Fields differ by generator. MABS never fills in property facts you didn't provide: missing details are left out.

  4. 04

    Step 4 · Generate, review and use

    Create Content You Can Work With

    MABS runs your details through its AI generation workflow and returns a draft for you to review. From there, the content is yours to shape.

    Review the result, verify the details, refine the message, and put your content to work.

    With every draft you can

    • Edit the text directly in MABS
    • Copy it and use it wherever you need it
    • Save it to the project and find it in your Content Library
    • Regenerate for a fresh version
    • Repurpose it as another format, such as a post, an email or a video script

    A draft is a starting point. Check it for accuracy and suitability before you publish. MABS does not publish to websites or social media.

Brand-aware by design

More Context. More Relevant Content.

A general AI tool only knows what you type into a single prompt. MABS works from a structured workflow that can draw on what you've already told it.

A single prompt in a general AI tool

  • You re-explain your business every time
  • You decide which details to include, and what gets missed
  • Results depend on how the prompt is written
  • Facts and assumptions can mix unless you check closely

A MABS generator

  • Your Brand Context is available to the generator
  • A form asks for the details this task needs
  • Property facts come from your project or the form
  • Each generator is focused on one marketing goal

  • Property details keep listing content grounded in the facts you provide.

  • Specialized generators organize the work around one marketing goal.

  • You review and approve the final content.

Frequently Asked Questions

More questions
What is MABS?

MABS, short for Megmos AI Brand Studio, is an AI-powered marketing platform focused on helping real estate professionals create content through specialized generators and structured workflows.

Do I need to provide all my business information first?

No. Start with the information the generator you choose asks for. Adding your Brand Context in Settings can make results more specific, and you can do that at any time.

Can I use MABS for different marketing tasks?

Yes. MABS has specialized generators for property descriptions, social content, email marketing and website copy. The free plan includes the Starter generators; the others will come with paid plans, which are coming soon.

Can I publish the generated content immediately?

Generated content should be reviewed and verified before publication. Check all property facts, contact details, claims, and other important information.

Does MABS automatically publish content to my website or social media?

No. MABS generates content for you to review, and you copy it wherever you need it. It does not publish to websites or social media, or connect to other platforms.

Can I generate content in Arabic and English?

Yes. MABS works in English and Arabic, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic, and generators write content in the language you're using in the app.
