Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Effective date: 13 November 2026

At Megmos LLC, trading as Megmos Creative Co. ("Company," "Megmos," "we," "us," or "our"), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect information when you use MABS (Megmos AI Brand Studio) at mabs.megmos.com, our website megmos.com, and related services (together, the "Service").

MABS is an AI-powered brand and marketing platform that helps real estate professionals and businesses create, organize and manage marketing content and branded materials. This policy covers:

  • Our websites at https://megmos.com and https://mabs.megmos.com.
  • Your MABS account and workspace.
  • AI-powered content, image, video and voice-over tools.
  • Brand profiles and business information.
  • Property information and marketing projects.
  • Templates, digital resources and downloadable materials.
  • Related communications, support and services.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Use (https://megmos.com/terms/) and Cookie Policy (https://megmos.com/cookies/). If you do not agree with our privacy practices, you should stop using the Service.

1. Who we are

MABS is operated by Megmos LLC, trading as Megmos Creative Co., registered in the State of Wyoming, United States. Mailing address: 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Privacy inquiries: privacy@megmos.com.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, Megmos LLC is the data controller for personal information processed in connection with its own operation of MABS. For content you create about your own clients (for example a client follow-up email), you decide what to enter and how to use the result, and we process that information only to provide the Service to you.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of MABS, and certain information from our service providers.

2.1 Account and registration information

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your password, which our authentication provider stores only in hashed form, never in plain text.
  • Your account ID, plan, trial or subscription status, and the dates your account was created and last signed in.
  • Your consent choices made at sign-up (Terms, Privacy Policy and, if you opt in, marketing emails), with the date.
  • A personal invitation code, if you joined through a pilot invite.

2.2 Business and brand information (Brand Context)

The business details you choose to add, such as your brand or agent name, brokerage, phone, email, website, office address, service areas, brand voice, social media links, brand colours and fonts, your logo and your headshot.

2.3 Property and project information

The details you enter about properties and marketing projects, such as addresses, descriptions, prices, specifications, features, neighbourhood information, and the photos and video clips you upload.

Please make sure you are authorized to provide property information, photographs and other materials. Avoid entering personal information about property owners, buyers, tenants or other people unless it is necessary, lawful and authorized.

2.4 AI inputs and generated content

  • The instructions, text and source content you enter into generators and studios.
  • The voice-over lines you write in Video Studio.
  • The content MABS generates for you (text, designs, images, videos and voice-overs), and the edits and saved versions you keep.
  • Usage records: which tools you run, when, and whether a generation succeeded. We use these to apply your plan's monthly limits and video credits and to fix errors.

2.5 Payment and subscription information

MABS does not take online payments yet. When paid plans launch, payments will be processed by Stripe. We will then keep your plan, billing status, payment transaction identifiers, payment and refund history, and billing dates. We will not store full card numbers or security codes on MABS systems: Stripe collects and processes them directly under its own privacy policy.

2.6 Technical and usage information

When you use the Service, our hosting and security providers automatically collect your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, request times, pages requested, error reports, and security and sign-in logs. We use these to deliver, secure and fix the Service.

2.7 Communications and support

If you contact us by email or through the contact form on megmos.com, we collect your name, email address and message. If you use the MABS Assistant chat on megmos.com, your questions are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply; we do not store them.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information only for the purposes below. Where EU or UK data protection law applies, the legal basis for each is shown in section 11.

3.1 Providing the Service

To create and manage your account, sign you in, keep your workspace, projects and Brand Context, generate the content you ask for, apply plan limits and video credits, provide downloads, and manage your plan.

3.2 Improving MABS

To find and fix errors, monitor performance and reliability, improve features and develop new ones, using technical and usage information. Where possible we use aggregated or de-identified information. We do not use your content to train AI models.

3.3 Account and customer communications

To send messages needed to run your account: sign-up confirmation, password resets, security alerts, trial and subscription notices, billing updates once paid plans launch, important changes to our policies, and replies to your support requests.

3.4 Marketing communications

Only if you opted in, to send product news, tips, new templates or offers. You can change this at any time in MABS under Settings → Privacy → Marketing Preferences, or with the unsubscribe link in any such email. We do not send marketing emails today. Unsubscribing does not stop essential account and security messages.

3.5 Security and fraud prevention

To detect suspicious activity, block bots and automated abuse, protect accounts, enforce our Terms of Use, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we process information with artificial intelligence

AI is central to MABS. When you use an AI-powered feature, the information needed for that request is sent to the relevant provider, which returns the result to us:

  • Text generation (Anthropic, Claude models): the generator's instructions, together with the relevant inputs, project facts and Brand Context. Also used for replies from the MABS Assistant on megmos.com.
  • Voice-overs (ElevenLabs): the voice-over lines you write, turned into speech when you choose a voice-over.
  • Video rendering (Shotstack): the photos, clips, texts, music and voice-over you choose for a video.

4.1 AI training and data use

Under Anthropic's published policy for commercial API customers, inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default, and are generally deleted within 30 days, subject to exceptions and any different contractual arrangements. Other providers process data under their own agreements, settings and retention policies, and we do not claim that every provider excludes all data from every form of retention or model improvement. We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not sell your personal information.

4.2 Review of AI-generated content

AI-generated content can contain errors or information that needs checking. You are responsible for reviewing it before you publish or share it. Voice-overs are synthetic (AI) voices.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only as described below.

5.2 Business transfers

If Megmos LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with the law or a lawful request from authorities, protect our rights and property, investigate fraud or unlawful activity, enforce our Terms of Use, or protect the safety of users and others.

5.4 With your consent

We may share information with others when you specifically ask us to.

6. International data transfers

MABS is operated by Megmos LLC in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on adequacy decisions (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified), the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, unless the law requires or permits longer:

  • Account data, Brand Context, projects and saved content: while your account is open. After account deletion, we delete it within 30 days, except where retention is legally required or permitted.
  • Backups: copies containing deleted data are removed through the backup cycle within a further 30 days, subject to technical limits and legal requirements.
  • Usage records: up to 24 months, for plan limits, security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
  • Abuse-prevention counters (Upstash): about one minute.
  • Hosting and security logs: according to our hosting provider's settings, generally days to weeks.
  • Payment records (once paid plans launch): as long as accounting, tax and legal obligations require.
  • Support messages: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless the law requires a different period.

Our service providers may keep information under their own contractual terms and retention policies. Deleting your account does not immediately remove information from backups or records we must keep by law.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

MABS uses only the cookies it needs to work (for example to keep you signed in) and one to remember your language. We do not use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies today. If we add any, we will ask for your consent first where the law requires it. Full details are in our Cookie Policy: https://megmos.com/cookies/

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure sign-in, access restrictions, providers with recognized security programmes, logging, software updates and backups. No system is completely secure. Please keep your password confidential and tell us if you suspect unauthorized access. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information and learn whether we process it.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability: receive a copy in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, or to direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.
  • Complain to your data protection authority.

To use these rights, email privacy@megmos.com, or send an export or deletion request from Settings → Privacy in MABS. These requests are handled manually for now. We may ask for information to verify your identity, and we reply within one month or within the deadline your law sets.

12. Additional information for United States residents

Depending on your state, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, you may have the right to know what categories of personal information we collect, to access, delete and correct it, to receive a portable copy, to opt out of sales, sharing or targeted advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. To submit a request, email privacy@megmos.com. We may need to verify your identity, and where the law allows, you may use an authorized agent.

13. Children's privacy

MABS is a professional tool for adults. Users must be at least 18 years old under our Terms of Use. The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact privacy@megmos.com and we will delete it.

14. Third-party websites and services

MABS may link to or embed third-party websites and services, such as YouTube or social networks. Their own privacy policies and terms apply, and we are not responsible for their practices. Please review them before giving them personal information.

15. Business and property information you upload

You are responsible for having the right to upload information about properties, businesses, listings and campaigns, and for making sure it does not violate anyone's privacy or other rights. Do not upload confidential client records, government ID documents, financial account details or other sensitive personal information to MABS. If you provide personal information about another person, you are responsible for having a legal basis and giving any notice the law requires.

16. Do Not Track and similar signals

We do not use tracking for advertising and do not sell or share personal information, so there is no such processing for browser signals like Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to switch off. If that changes, we will honor recognized opt-out preference signals where the law requires.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to MABS, new providers or technology, changes in our practices, or legal requirements. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify you through the Service or by email before they take effect.
