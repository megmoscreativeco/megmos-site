This Cookie Policy explains how Megmos LLC, trading as Megmos Creative Co. ("Company," "Megmos," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on megmos.com and in MABS (Megmos AI Brand Studio) at mabs.megmos.com.

In short: we use only what the Service needs to work and to remember your language. We do not use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your computer, phone or other device. They let a site recognize your browser, keep you signed in and remember your choices.

  • Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay for a set period or until you delete them.
  • First-party cookies are set by the site you are visiting.
  • Third-party cookies are set by another provider whose service is built into that site.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Turnstile (sign-up and sign-in pages): checks that you are a person and not a bot. It runs in a frame from challenges.cloudflare.com and is handled under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • YouTube (Help Center tutorial videos): videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only when you click play. Once a video plays, YouTube may store cookies or local storage on your device under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (megmos.com): delivers the site's fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. No cookies are set.

We do not control what independent providers do. Please see their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Consent and your preferences

Because we use only strictly necessary and functional items that you request, we do not show a cookie banner. You can see everything MABS uses under Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences.

Before we add any non-essential cookie or similar technology, such as analytics, we will ask for your consent where the law requires it, with a tool that lets you accept, reject or customize your choices and change them at any time. Your choice will be remembered on later visits.

6. How to manage cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, delete and block cookies, block third-party cookies, or warn you when a cookie is set. If you block the sign-in cookie, you will not be able to sign in to MABS.

7. Cookies and personal information

Some of these items, such as IP addresses, session identifiers and browser details, can be personal information. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle it. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.

8. International users

MABS is operated by Megmos LLC, registered in the State of Wyoming, United States, and is used in many countries. We follow applicable cookie and electronic communications rules, including those of the EU and UK. Where they apply, non-essential cookies will be used only with your consent.
