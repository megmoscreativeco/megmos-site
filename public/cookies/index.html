Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on megmos.com and in the MABS app (mabs.megmos.com). A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar store that stays on your device.

megmos.com

megmos.com does not set any cookies. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

To display its fonts, the site loads them from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles this.

The MABS app

We use only what MABS needs to work and to remember your choices:

NameTypePurposeDurationCategory
sb-…-auth-tokenCookie (Supabase)Keeps you signed in securely (may be split into numbered parts)Until you sign out, or the session expiresStrictly necessary
MABS_LOCALECookieRemembers your language (English or Arabic)1 yearFunctional
mabs_cookie_prefsLocal storageRemembers your cookie choicesUntil you clear itStrictly necessary

Strictly necessary items cannot be switched off, because the app cannot work without them. We do not use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies today. If we add any, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Manage your preferences in MABS under Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you will not be able to use MABS.
